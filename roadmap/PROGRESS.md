# DSA Mastery Progress

> **Do not mark a problem mastered because an old accepted solution exists.** Mark it only after a cold solve that meets the interview standard.

## Mastery scale

- **0 — Unfamiliar:** cannot classify the problem.
- **1 — Recognition:** recognize the pattern after a hint.
- **2 — Assisted:** can solve after reviewing notes/solution.
- **3 — Independent:** cold solve succeeds, but slowly or with bugs.
- **4 — Interview-ready:** cold solve in target time with clean explanation/testing.
- **5 — Durable:** still interview-ready after spaced reviews.

Recommended target by end of Week 8: core Mediums at **4+**, with durable recall trending toward **5**.
